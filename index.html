Urbanization &
Environmental Change

My research examines how cities grow and how that growth changes land, water and climate. Combining GIS, remote sensing, field-based investigation and modelling, I study patterns of urban expansion and land-use/land-cover change, and what they mean for hydrology, biodiversity and people.

Md. Humayun Kabir is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Environmental Science at Stamford University Bangladesh. He holds B.Sc. and M.Sc. degrees in Geography and Environment from Jahangirnagar University, Bangladesh.
